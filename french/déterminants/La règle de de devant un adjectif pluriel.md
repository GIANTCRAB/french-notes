En français, 

Whenever you have the plural article **des** (whether indefinite or partitive), and it is immediately followed by an **adjective** that comes **before** a plural noun, you must change **des** to **de** (or **d'** if the adjective starts with a vowel).

# Par exemples

- _Des_ chaussures -> **De** belles chaussures
- _Des_ idées -> **D'**excellentes idées
- _Des_ vacances -> **De** bonnes vacances
